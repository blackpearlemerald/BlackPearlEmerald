GBA SPRITE FORMAT NOTES
-----------------------
For pokeemerald-expansion, overworld sprites must be:
  - 16x32 pixels per frame (one frame = one direction step)
  - 4 frames per row x 4 direction rows = 64x128 total spritesheet
  - OR the standard FRLG layout used by object_event_graphics.c

Battle trainer sprites:
  - 64x64 pixels, indexed PNG, max 16 colours
  - Placed in graphics/trainers/pics/<name>.png
  - Referenced via TRAINER_PIC_* enum in include/constants/trainers.h
